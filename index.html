Frequently Asked Questions

What will actually change in 28 days?
You'll know what to eat, when and how much for your body. You'll have four habits you've done every day for four weeks. And you'll have proven to yourself that you can stay consistent, which is the thing that's been missing.
What if work gets busy or life gets in the way?
That's what the program is built for. A dietitian or clinical nutritionist reviews your meals every week and adjusts them to what's actually going on.
I've got upcoming plans. Can I still do it?
Yes. Tell your dietitian or clinical nutritionist when you start and the plan is built around them. The habits are small enough to travel with, and one weekend doesn't undo four weeks.
How strict is the meal plan? Do I have to give up the foods I like?
No. It's healthy meals, not a full restriction plan. Your meal plan is built by a dietitian or clinical nutritionist around your body, your symptoms and your dietary needs, and if something doesn't work for you, it gets swapped.
How often do I hear from the health specialist?
Your health specialist checks in regularly throughout the 28 days. On top of that you've got chat access, a weekly plan review, and two live check-ins. You're never left wondering whether anyone's paying attention.
I've tried tracking apps, fasting and meal plans before. Why would this be different?
Because it's not an information problem. It's a personalisation and implementation problem. Having someone build a plan for your body and hold you accountable puts you in a much better position to start, and to stay consistent.
Is there a guarantee?
Yes. A 30-day money-back guarantee. If you don't feel you've had the value of the service, we'll refund you.
What happens after the 28 days?
We look at the next phase together. If you want to keep going, we'll build it with you. If not, the habits are yours.
Do I need a gym membership or equipment?
No. 5,000 steps a day is the only movement habit. If you already train, keep training. Nothing else is required.
